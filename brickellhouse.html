Brickell House

Brickell / 2014

46 Stories / 374 Residences of 1, 2 & 3 Bedrooms

1300 Brickell Bay Dr Miami, FL 33131, USA +1.800.531.9572

The Sieger Suarez team and the Newgard Development Group brought together a luxurious condo residential building to the Brickell area. Yabu Pushelberg, the famous interior designer brought his essence to this luxury tower, combining New York elegance and urban atmosphere. The three story lobby, rooftop pool, and the two restaurants make this condo a destination place in Brickell. With 30 day rentals and spectacular Bay and City views, this is one of the best condo buildings of Brickell.
