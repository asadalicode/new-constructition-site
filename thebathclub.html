The Bath Club Miami Beach

Miami Beach / 2005

19 Stories / 112 Residences of 1, 2, 3, 4 & 5 Bedrooms

5959 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33140, USA

The Bath Club perfectly defines Miami Beach condo's must-haves: superb units, great and uncommon amenities, incomparable location and tropical ambiance. Formed in 1926 and perfected in 2005, this condominium is full of details and continually reinveinting itself. The level of the features, services and amenities is the finest, it also offers easy access to shops, cultural destinations, restaurants and nightlife. The common areas were built and decorated with a Mediterranean Revival flair.
