Diesel Wynwood

2006

8 Stories / 159 Residences of Studios, 1, 2 & 3 Bedrooms

148 NW 28th St, Miami, FL 33127, USA

Average Price: $1,300 x SF ($13,993 x M2)

Diesel Wynwood Residences offers a smart and paradigm-shifting living in the heart of Miami’s art-centric Wynwood District. The visionary team of Zyscovich Architects designed an 8-story building full of audacity and playfulness, with steel pillars which allow for pedestrians to enjoy an outdoor piazza. Overhead, neon structural beams form an iconic geometric symbol that enhances the building’s uniqueness and gives a glimpse into the exclusive design found within. The Diesel Wynwood building also boasts extensive amenities, including a pool, open-air art gallery, spa, meditation room, fitness center, art studio, co-working spaces and much more. Residences exceed expectations, with luxury design collaborations, high ceilings and sustainability. The building is located at the center of a premier destination for world-class dining, global entertainment, international events, art, fashion, design, innovation and creative enterprise.
